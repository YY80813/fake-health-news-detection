ℹ️ About This System

🤖 How It Works

Two-step pipeline: your fine-tuned PubMedBERT model classifies the article first, then an LLM independently rechecks it against official sources — the top banner shows whether the recheck confirms or contradicts your model's call

🌐 Official Source Check

Verification is scoped to official outlets only: BBC Health, Malaysia's Ministry of Health (KKM), WHO and CDC — not general web results

📎 Cited Evidence

Every verdict comes with links to the specific official pages the model used, so you can check the sources yourself

🤖 Your Trained Model(s)

PubMedBERT and BioBERT were both fine-tuned on the HealthStory dataset and compared in this project. Pick either one, or "Compare both", above the article box — the chosen model(s) run alongside the LLM check and give their own fake/real prediction with a confidence score

⚠️ Disclaimer

For research purposes only. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice.
